GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Clear information matters—especially in healthcare procurement.

Does MediNitor currently stock every product on this site?

No. The portfolio shows planned sourcing categories. Specific inventory, configuration, availability and commercial arrangements must be confirmed before any order.

Is MediNitor an authorized distributor of the listed manufacturers?

No authorization is claimed. The manufacturer section offers independent research references. Any future verified appointments will be published with their actual scope.

Can MediNitor supply medical reagents and rapid test kits?

These are intended product categories. Supply would depend on specific product availability, verified manufacturer arrangements, applicable DGDA requirements, and other import approvals.

How will quotations and product documentation work?

Product-specific inquiries should include the intended application and specifications. Technical documents, pricing, licensing and availability should be checked directly before making commercial commitments.
